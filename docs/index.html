Inside

Built for focus.

Didi’s House is a small, professionally sound-treated studio in Kensington B, Randburg. A proper booth with good mics, close to Sandton, Bryanston, Fourways, Randburg, Rosebank and most of Joburg north.

Johannesburg has brilliant full-scale studios, and if you need an engineer behind glass and a live room, book one of those. If you need a clean, quiet, treated room with good equipment at a fair hourly rate, this is that space.

Bring your own laptop and DAW; everything else is set up when you arrive. If you’d like a hand with a simple recording or a basic edit, just ask.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Do I need to bring my own laptop?
Yes. Bring a laptop with the DAW you like to use (Reaper, Audacity, Logic, Pro Tools, Adobe Audition and so on). The Audient iD14 connects over USB. If you’d rather not drive the software yourself, say so when you book and I’ll help set up a simple recording.
What’s included in the hourly rate?
Everything in the room: the treated booth, AKG P220 condenser mic, Shure SM58 dynamic mic, Audient iD14 interface, Audio-Technica ATH-M20X headphones, Akai MPK Mini MK3, stands, cables and pop filter, plus WiFi, off-street parking, and coffee and tea.
How does the audiobook half-price deal work?
Audiobooks recorded in any South African language book at R350 per hour instead of R700. WhatsApp the title and rough length to confirm the rate before your first session.
Is there parking at the studio?
Yes, free off-street parking on the property.
Where is the studio and when can I book?
Kensington B, Randburg, easy to reach from Sandton, Northcliff, Bryanston, Rosebank, Fourways and most of Joburg north. Sessions run on weekdays between 8am and 5pm, by appointment. The full address comes with your booking confirmation.
